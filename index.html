ClothingERP · Windows desktop application

Your business records.
A backup you control.

ClothingERP brings billing, inventory, purchases and reports together. Its optional backup feature keeps an encrypted copy of your database on your own drive or in your Google Drive account.

How backups workRead our privacy policy
01 / CHOOSE

Your destination

Select a local folder, external drive or pen drive. Optionally connect your Google account from the ERP to store an additional copy in Google Drive.

02 / PROTECT

Your passphrase

A recovery passphrase encrypts your backup before it is copied to your chosen destination. Keep that passphrase safely outside the computer.

03 / BACK UP

Your schedule

Run a backup yourself or choose a schedule. Scheduled backups require the desktop application and local API to be running with the designated operator signed in.

Why we ask for Google Drive access

ClothingERP Backups uploads encrypted .cerpbak files to a ClothingERP Backups folder in the account you connect. It checks upload results and manages older backups according to your retention setting.

The app requests the limited drive.file permission for files it creates or you specifically authorize. It reads your connected account's email to show which account is in use. It does not request access to all of your Drive files, Gmail messages or contacts.

Sign-in takes place in Google's browser page. You never enter your Google password into ClothingERP. See the privacy policy for data handling, retention and disconnection details.
