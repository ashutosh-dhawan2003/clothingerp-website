Using the backup feature

Terms of use

Effective date: 18 October 2026

These terms describe use of ClothingERP Backups, the optional local and Google Drive backup feature of ClothingERP, developed by Ashutosh Dhawan.

Authorized use

Use the feature only for databases, storage destinations and Google accounts you are authorized to access. The designated ERP operator is responsible for enabling backups and choosing the appropriate destination. Database archives may include personal, business and authentication records.

Your backup responsibilities

Keep the recovery passphrase securely outside the computer, review backup success messages, maintain adequate destination storage, and periodically test recovery. A copy on the same physical disk does not protect against failure of that disk. Changing a passphrase does not change the passphrase required by older archives.

Scheduled backups require the desktop application and local API to be running with the designated operator signed in. Backup completion depends on the computer, database, storage permissions, network and Google account connection. No uninterrupted availability or recovery of every change is promised.

Google Drive and local storage

The Google connection is optional and subject to Google's terms, authorization rules and storage limits. The ERP does not purchase extra Google storage on your behalf. You may disconnect the account and manage or delete your backup copies. Review the privacy policy for permissions, retention and revocation details.

Recovery

Restoring a database requires the correct passphrase, a valid archive and a compatible SQL Server installation. Extraction produces an unencrypted database backup that must be kept private. Verify a restored copy before replacing any working database. Files and configuration outside the database require separate backup arrangements.
