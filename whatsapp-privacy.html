WhatsApp integration

Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy describes ClothingERP's optional WhatsApp messaging integration and the Cloudflare delivery relay used with the local ERP installation. The developer and privacy contact is Ashutosh Dhawan, at ashutoshdhawan012003@gmail.com. The business using the ERP manages its own customer records and decides which messages to send. This policy supplements the separate Google Drive backup policy.

Information used and why

The integration uses customer names and phone numbers, recorded messaging permissions and opt-outs, approved message templates, invoice details and PDF attachments to send messages selected by an authorized ERP operator. It records queued messages, provider message identifiers, delivery states and failure codes to track sending and diagnose failures.

Business account and sender identifiers and Meta authorization tokens connect the business's WhatsApp account. Dashboard-saved tokens are encrypted on the ERP API host and are not returned in dashboard responses. Credentials configured manually are also subject to the installation administrator's file and machine security.

Meta and WhatsApp

When an operator sends a message, the ERP transfers the recipient's phone number, the message template and its values, and any selected invoice PDF or media to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform over HTTPS. Meta processes that information to provide its services. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. Connecting an account does not itself send an invoice.

Cloudflare delivery relay

When the messages webhook is enabled, Meta sends message-status events and incoming-message events to the configured Cloudflare Worker. The Worker checks the request signature and limits retained information to delivery identifiers, delivery states, failure codes and explicit opt-out requests for the configured business phone number. Ordinary incoming message text is not stored by the relay. Opt-out records retain the sender's phone number and timestamp so the ERP can stop further messages.

The local ERP retrieves these records over an authenticated HTTPS connection and acknowledges them after saving them locally. Acknowledged records are removed from the relay. Unacknowledged records expire after seven days and are removed by an hourly cleanup. Cloudflare may process technical request and security information under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. This relay serves the configured installation; separate businesses must not share its credentials.

Local storage and access

Customer records, message drafts, attachments, permissions and messaging history are held on the ERP installation's storage. Access is controlled by the installation's accounts, roles and host security. Local records do not automatically expire when the relay deletes its copy. The business operating the installation manages local retention and backups according to its needs and applicable obligations.

Your choices and deletion requests

Contact the business that sent a message to request correction or deletion of its customer records or to withdraw messaging permission. You can also reply STOP to the business's WhatsApp number. Automated processing of that reply depends on the webhook and ERP connection working; contact the business directly if messages continue.

For assistance with ClothingERP or deletion of information held by the developer, email ashutoshdhawan012003@gmail.com with the subject “ClothingERP privacy request”. Identify the business and the information concerned, but do not send access tokens, passwords, backup archives or identity documents. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the request. The developer cannot directly remove records stored exclusively on a customer's computer or in a customer's backup account. Deletion from the ERP does not automatically remove messages already delivered to recipients or data retained independently by service providers.

An authorized administrator can disconnect the WhatsApp connection in the ERP and revoke the app's access through Meta. Disconnecting does not erase existing business records, backups or delivered messages.

Website and support

This informational page is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no developer-added advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email support, the address and information you provide are used to respond to your request.

Updates

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date. Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact above.
