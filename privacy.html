Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This policy describes the Google Drive backup integration in the ClothingERP Windows application, named ClothingERP Backups, and this informational website. The developer and support contact is Ashutosh Dhawan: ashutoshdhawan012003@gmail.com.

Information the backup feature handles

  • Business database: the backup is a whole-database copy. It can include customer and employee details, invoices, purchases, inventory, rewards, financial records and ERP authentication records held in your installation.
  • Google account information: the connected account's email address is read from Google Drive to display the active backup account.
  • Authorization information: Google OAuth access and refresh tokens allow the application to perform the backup operations you enable.
  • Backup metadata and settings: file names, identifiers, sizes, checksums, creation times, an installation identifier, destination settings, retention preferences and success or failure status are used to upload, verify and manage backups.

Google permissions and their purpose

The application requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits access to files created by the app or explicitly authorized by you, rather than general access to every file in your Drive.

The integration creates its backup folder, uploads encrypted archives, reads relevant file metadata to confirm uploads, and moves older backups from this installation to Drive trash according to your retention setting. Google account data is used only to provide and identify the backup connection. The integration does not read Gmail messages or Google contacts.

Storage, transfer and protection

The local ERP API creates a SQL Server backup. The desktop application encrypts it using AES-256-GCM before saving it to your selected local destination or uploading it over HTTPS to your Google Drive. SQL Server needs a temporary unencrypted staging file; normal completion or cancellation removes it, but an interrupted process can leave a file requiring administrator cleanup.

Saved Google authorization and backup settings, including the recovery passphrase used for scheduled backups, are protected with Windows current-user data protection on your computer. Your Google password is entered only on Google's sign-in page and is not collected by the ERP.

The backup integration does not send your database archive or Google tokens to a developer-operated cloud server. Google receives the encrypted archive and associated metadata when you enable Drive backup. Google handles its services under the Google Privacy Policy. Local files remain on storage you choose.

Keep a separate, secure copy of the recovery passphrase. It is required to decrypt a backup on another computer; Google sign-in and developer support cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Use and sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, used to build advertising profiles, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence models. The backup integration does not provide the developer with routine access to the contents of your backups. Data is transferred to Google only to provide the Drive backup functionality you select.

ClothingERP Backups' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

If you contact support by email, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Do not send credentials, recovery passphrases or backup archives.

Retention and deletion

You choose the number of backup copies to retain. After a new backup is verified, the app removes older matching local backups or moves older matching Google Drive backups from this installation to trash. Files in Drive trash may continue to consume storage until deleted permanently.

You can disable scheduled backups, deselect Google uploads, or disconnect the Google account in the ERP. Disconnecting removes locally stored Google authorization and account association; it does not delete backups already stored in Drive or revoke access for other installations.

To revoke Google's authorization, remove ClothingERP Backups from your Google Account connections. Delete existing archives directly from Google Drive and empty trash if you want permanent removal. Local archives can be deleted from their destination folders. Removing backups does not delete records from the live ERP database.

Other local backup settings remain on the computer until removed. For help removing local settings or requesting deletion of support correspondence, contact the email below. Support cannot directly delete backups held exclusively in your own Drive or local storage.

This website

This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in, backup upload form, advertising scripts or analytics added by the developer. GitHub may process technical information such as visitor IP addresses as part of hosting and security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website does not receive your ERP backups or Google tokens.
